### Additional Verified Supporting References Cited in `literature_review.md`
- **Martin, B. (2007)** — *On the Complexity of a Derivative Chess Problem* (`arXiv:cs/0701049`, `W1677836423`, Green OA): Proves NP-completeness of the exact Walled N-Queens variant (squares that stop queen attacks).
- **Chatham, D. (2016)** — *The Maximum Queens Problem with Pawns* (*Recreational Mathematics Magazine*, 6, 95–102; `W2560327409`, `10.1515/rmm-2016-0010`, Hybrid OA): Establishes the $\lceil N/2 \rceil^2$ maximum-queens bound with walls/pawns and row-segment upper bounds.
- **Simkin, M. (2023)** — *The number of n-queens configurations* (*Advances in Mathematics*, 427, 109127; `arXiv:2107.13460`, `W3185963146`, `10.1016/j.aim.2023.109127`, Green OA): Establishes $\mathcal{Q}(n) = ((1\pm o(1))ne^{-\alpha})^n$ ($1.94 < \alpha < 1.9449$), framing output-volume scaling.
- **Sosič, R., & Gu, J. (1990)** — *A polynomial time algorithm for the N-Queens problem* (*ACM SIGART Bulletin*, 1(3), 7–11; `W2042038305`, `10.1145/101340.101343`, Bronze OA): Contrasts fast single-solution local search ($N \le 500{,}000$) against full-enumeration exact search.
- **Nishino, M., Yasuda, N., Minato, S., & Nagata, M. (2017)** — *Dancing with Decision Diagrams: A Combined Approach to Exact Cover* (*AAAI 2017*; `W2604444013`, `10.1609/aaai.v31i1.10662`, Diamond OA): Evaluates ZDD-memoized DLX trade-offs versus direct backtracking.
- **Lam, T. T., Nguyen, V. C., & Nguyen, X. H. G. (2026)** — *Search space representation and analysis of the backtracking algorithm for the N-Queens problem* (*Dong Thap University Journal of Science*; `W7213524069`, `10.52714/dthu.ns.3283.2031`, Diamond OA): Empirical small-$N$ ($N=8, 10$) comparison of permutation, array backtracking, and bitmask backtracking.
